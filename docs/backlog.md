# Backlog

Known problems and loose ends that are not being worked on right now. Newest first.

## Bugs

- **Romulan ship selector: fixed in v177.** The Romulan, Kzinti and Tholian race lists held the Q-ships of the race they borrow from (R1.7A), so clicking one switched the tab to Gorn, Klingon or Federation. Q-ships are now listed only under the Federation, Klingon and Gorn. The SG4 convoy Q-ship option still gives a Romulan, Kzinti or Tholian game the borrowed Q-ship (R1.7A), because it picks it by race, not from the list.

## Open items from the scenario work

- **Orion option mounts and OAKDISC (v176): what was not built and why.** Built for the Orion Raider Cruiser only (the only Orion ship in the game), behind a Master Rulebook switch. Not built: Phaser-G and Plasma-G/S (G and S need two adjacent centerline mounts, which the CR does not have, and Phaser-G is not in the game); Disruptor-40 (the disruptor range table stops at 30); Aux, AWR, Repair, barracks and Drone rack E/G options; choosing FA instead of the default wing arcs for disruptors (R8.R3); the swivel plasma mount is just given the wider forward plasma arc (swivel is not modelled); the cartel availability tables (G15.44) and tactical intelligence to reveal the pirate's mounts (SG6.61), so computer pirates roll a rough table and you can see what they carry; the Y175 type-C drone rack conversion (R8.R2; no year selection yet); sabot and plasma carronades. The mounts are drawn in the three boxes at the bottom of the (synthetic) CR sheet. Mount weapon BPV costs are fractions (Phaser-2 -0.25, Phaser-3 -0.5), so a CR can have a BPV like 92.5.
- **Starbase (SB, v180): what was not built and why.** Built: five racial starbases (Federation, Klingon, Kzinti, Gorn, Romulan; `data/starbase.js`, generated from the SSD book page 24 and the R1.1A weapon table, `js/28e-starbase.js`), six shields of 50 boxes (Y170 refit: 70, Y175 refit: 80, with the extra armour boxes), BPV 600/650/675, 12 Ph-4 and six W2 Ph-3, the race's W1 and W4 weapons, rotation rate 4 (C3.72), seeking-weapon control of twice the sensor rating (R1.1A), and the full SG8.61 fleets with the SG8.2 balance, SG8.62 and SG8.63-size options. Not built:
  - The R1.1D damage procedure (shield faces a docking module, cargo hits scored on docked ships, excess damage into the core, armour rows by shield). Hits use the ordinary damage allocation chart against the whole base, with the SSD's armour boxes as an Armor system. Docked ships, the hangar, PF and power augmentation modules and docked pods (R1.1B, R1.1F, C13.6 to C13.8) are not built; R1.1B1 says to ignore the augmentation modules in the Basic Set.
  - The SSD sheet is a flat, synthetic layout (one block per system), not the six drawn docking modules; the module boxes (Aux, Brdg, Btty and so on) are totals for six modules. Counts were read from the image and are approximate: APR 52 core boxes plus 36 AWR boxes (all 88 are treated as AWR-capable so the base can arm photons, H4.3), Cargo 28 plus 180 repair boxes (repair boxes are cargo in the Basic Set), 12 shuttles (hangar module shuttles ignored), 12 tractors, 24 batteries, 16 excess damage boxes.
  - Weapon arcs are all 360 degrees (the SSD shows no arcs and R1.1 is silent on them). W2: Ph-G does not exist in the game, so six Ph-3 are used (the R1.1A note allows this before the weapon was invented). W3: Gorn and Romulan Plasma-D racks are not built; six 360-degree Ph-3 stand in (the R1.1A note for before Y165, so a small departure from Y168). Type-H drone racks hold 20 drones in one rack and launch one per turn (FD3.8 to FD3.84: the five magazines, the type-VI magazine, magazine damage and four-impulse switching are not modelled); ADD-30s hold 30 anti-drones (FD3.86, E5.53: magazine switching not modelled).
  - Not built: limited and full aegis (D13.4, D13.0; the game has no aegis rules); Klingon UIM modules, security stations and flag bridges; the Federation Y181 refit (+20, 12-fighter bay); the Romulan cloak (+90 BPV); the 24 base fighters and PF/escort ships (R1.1C, R1.1E); special sensors dying on phaser damage points; the 4 probes launch as one probe type only.
  - Explosion strength 30 is an estimate (the Master Ship Chart row is not in the Basic Set text). Crew 250 and 50 boarding parties come from the Master Ship Chart (the SSD sheet draws 300 crew boxes).
  - The map token and 3D model are the Battle Station's (no Starbase drawing yet).
  - SG8: rotation rate is fixed at 4 (the player could choose, C3.71); the flagship command limit, drone speed agreement (SG8.432), refit agreement (SG8.44 beyond the base's own refits), the two-race alliance (SG8.63), the balance options SG8.71 to SG8.73 (swap ships, add ships to the base's side) and year selection are not built. The computer base fires everything it can; it has not been tuned (a Gorn or Romulan base with 12 plasma torpedoes kills a lone flagship quickly).
- **v175 scenarios: what was not built and why.**
  - SG8 Assault on a Starbase: the real Starbase and the SG8.61 fleets were built in v180 (see the Starbase entry below). Attackers still always enter from the west edge at speed 10 (the rules let the player choose any edge or two adjacent edges and the speed).
  - SG9 A Very Special Ally: not built. It needs two competing ships to collect research points separately, a shared hidden S6.1 solution draw, the communication roll (SG9.47) and player control of the monster (SG9.48); the monster code today (`19-monsters.js`, global `EEL`) tracks information for one human ship only. Needs a rework of `EEL.info` to per-ship before it can be done.
  - SH2 The Surprise Reversed: "WB+" is flown as a plain WB (no WB refit exists in this game). The Neutral Zone is a straight dashed line approximating hexes 0126 to 4205. Not enforced: half boarding parties, fire control that cannot be turned off, tractors and transporters while surprised (only the energy allocation is restricted). The "fired" trigger counts any Federation shot (not a launched drone that has not hit yet). The CC's special drones (SH2.432) and the optional MRS shuttles are not offered.
  - SH3 The Coming of the Meteor: the meteor cannot be shot at, so SH3.47 (400 points of damage break it into fragments, and the fragment victory results) is not built; the Klingon withdrawal declaration (SH3.48) is not built either (the computer never makes it). The meteor is a marker, not a moon hex: ships can fly through it only by being destroyed (SH3.45). Towing needs the tractor option switched on, which the scenario does for you.
  - SH4 Cruise Drones: the second-player option (SH4.1) and the F5's special drones (SH4.431, SH4.432) are not built. The freighter counts as lost if it is dead in space as well as destroyed. The freighter is flown straight at the 42xx column and never dodges.
- Fighters (v174) are the Kzinti AAS only: no rearming or repair aboard (J4.8), no dogfights (J7), fighters fire only at ships, the computer flies all fighters, and a computer carrier never slows to recover them. Other fighters and carriers (CVS, AFF, auxiliary carrier for SH5) are not built.
- SG3 (v174): the Tholian 2xPC lists, the third-player option and SG3.64 modules/minefields are not built.
- Not yet tested in a long game: the type-G rack anti-drone firing against real drone attacks (v174 tug block).
- Year selection (Y168 default) and Commander's Option Items (S3.2) are not built; they apply to every scenario.
- Ally and fleet AI: SG8 fleets of 10 to 11 ships run (v180); SG8 is the largest fleet in the game. Reinforcement arrival was built for SG3 in v174 (`sg3Rein`).
- See `docs/scenario-matrix.md` and `docs/rules-coverage-audit.md` for the full lists.
