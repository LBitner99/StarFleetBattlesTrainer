# Backlog

Known problems and loose ends that are not being worked on right now. Newest first.

## Bugs

- **Post-game strategy analysis (reported 2026-11-21; fixed in v183 and v184, audit still open).** The debrief advised HET (and high energy turns, sideslip, overloads) in a Basic Set game where those rules were off. v183 gated those pieces of advice; v184 writes the rules in force (preset, every option on and off) as the oldest line of the game log when the game starts, and the debrief reads them back from that log line (`rulesLine`, `ruleOn` in `js/33-debrief.js`), so changing options afterwards cannot change the advice. Still to do: audit every other debrief suggestion against the rules in force (so far only HET, sideslip, high energy turns and overloads are gated; the others were read and use Basic Set features only), and add the Master Rulebook and scenario options (landing, zones, cloak, legendary officers and so on) to the logged list.

- **Romulan ship selector: fixed in v177.** The Romulan, Kzinti and Tholian race lists held the Q-ships of the race they borrow from (R1.7A), so clicking one switched the tab to Gorn, Klingon or Federation. Q-ships are now listed only under the Federation, Klingon and Gorn. The SG4 convoy Q-ship option still gives a Romulan, Kzinti or Tholian game the borrowed Q-ship (R1.7A), because it picks it by race, not from the list.

## Open items from the scenario work

- **Orion option mounts and OAKDISC (v176): what was not built and why.** Built for the Orion Raider Cruiser only (the only Orion ship in the game), behind a Master Rulebook switch. Not built: Phaser-G and Plasma-G/S (G and S need two adjacent centerline mounts, which the CR does not have, and Phaser-G is not in the game); Disruptor-40 (the disruptor range table stops at 30); Aux, AWR, Repair, barracks and Drone rack E/G options; choosing FA instead of the default wing arcs for disruptors (R8.R3); the swivel plasma mount is just given the wider forward plasma arc (swivel is not modelled); the cartel availability tables (G15.44) and tactical intelligence to reveal the pirate's mounts (SG6.61), so computer pirates roll a rough table and you can see what they carry; the Y175 type-C drone rack conversion (R8.R2; no year selection yet); sabot and plasma carronades. The mounts are drawn in the three boxes at the bottom of the (synthetic) CR sheet. Mount weapon BPV costs are fractions (Phaser-2 -0.25, Phaser-3 -0.5), so a CR can have a BPV like 92.5.
- **Starbase damage (R1.1D, v182): what was not built and why.** (1) Cargo hits on a module cannot be scored on warships docked inside it (R1.1D 2A, C13.62): docking inside a starbase is not built, so after the repair boxes are gone the hits go to the next DAC column; in the rulebook example from hit 50 on, the book puts those hits on the docked D5. (2) Special sensors (R1.1E, scout functions): not on the sheet, so a phaser damage point never destroys one (hit 12 of the book example). (3) Augmentation modules and docked pods (R1.1B, R1.1D 4): not built, so step 4 goes only to docking modules whose armor is gone. (4) Which drone rack is lost: the rack count is kept as a total, so the highest-numbered rack is disabled, not the one in the module that was hit. (5) Klingon UIM and security stations beyond the one flag-bridge-style box per module, the Federation Y181 fighter bay, labs and other core functions have no game effect. (6) Core box counts (48 center hull, 28 cargo) were read from the SSD image and could be off by a few. (7) When a phaser hit scores on a module the owner's choice is not asked: the Ph-4 are lost first.
- **Starbase (SB, v180, corrected in v181): what was not built and why.** v181 correction: the second Starbase SSD page (SSD book page 25) was not read in v180.
