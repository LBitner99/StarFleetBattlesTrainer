# Rules coverage audit: SFB Duel game vs. the Silver Anniversary Master Rulebook

Date: 2026-11-20 (updated). Game build: v169; sections 6g to 6o cover v96 to v140 (the body of the audit was last fully re-read at v86, D5 explosions added on top of the v79 re-audit; re-audited after the rules-level, advanced-weapons, transporter/boarding, Orion and multi-ship work). Source: the Electronic Master Rulebook PDF (468 pages: Basic Set, Advanced Missions and the modules). Method: for each rule family the rulebook text was read and compared with what the game's code actually does; the numeric tables the book prints as images (turn mode chart, E1.7 and others) were checked on the rendered pages. "Not found" means I searched the code and found no implementation.

Limits of this audit:
- The Annexes (Master Ship Chart, weapon charts, drone and plasma tables, SSD data) are NOT in this PDF. Ship data (BPV, crew, breakdown ratings, movement cost) and the phaser/disruptor/photon/plasma/ADD tables were built from the Captain's Basic Set SSD book and could not be re-checked against the Master Rulebook. Re-check at v79: the game now cites 182 distinct rule numbers; 181 were found as "(code)" in the Master Rulebook text, the exception being SG1.41, which belongs to the scenario book.
- The level tags below are the ones printed in the book (Basic = untagged, Advanced, Commander's, Optional).

## 1. Checked against the master and found correct
- C3 turn mode chart and the category of all 26 ships in the game (A to E) match the chart on page 21.
- C2.21 acceleration limit (previous speed or ten, whichever is greater); C6.2/C6.3 HET cost 5, once per 8 impulses, not on impulse 1; C6.52 first-HET adjustment; C6.54 breakdown effects.
- D6.34 ECM/ECCM net shift table (it is the square root, as coded); E1.82 shifts above 6 move to the next range column.
- E4.21/E4.22/E4.41 photon arming (2+2), holding, overload; D9.21 to D9.23 damage control; C7.1/C7.53 disengagement by acceleration; C8 emergency deceleration; C5.1, C5.13, C5.2 tactical maneuvers; C9 flywheel (all of C9.1 to C9.3 that can occur in a duel); C4 sideslip.
- E1.7 small-target table, C10 erratic maneuvering (full text now in v37).

## 2. Differences found and fixed in this pass (v38)
1. C6.541: a breakdown must cancel the ship's tactical maneuvers, EM and unused HETs, and no tactical maneuver is allowed for the 16 post-breakdown impulses. Now done.
2. C6.521/C11.22: nimble ships (the Tholian PC and PC+) subtract 2 from the breakdown roll of their first AND second HET. Now done.
3. C6.522/S2.41: a crippled ship gets no first-HET adjustment. Now done (crippled tested on conditions A, C and E only).
4. C11.26/E1.7: nimble ships are harder to hit at range (natural ECM +2 at 15 to 29, +4 at 30+, not cumulative with EM). Now done.
5. E1.5: no weapon may fire twice within eight impulses, including across a turn boundary (a weapon fired on impulse 32 used to be able to fire again on impulse 1). Now done for phasers, photons and disruptors.
6. C10 erratic maneuvering rewritten from the real text (cost six movement hexes, 4 ECM both ways, start/stop, turn mode +1, HET +1, no launching, anti-drone +1, ends on emergency deceleration). C11.23: nimble EM cost three hexes.
7. Retracted earlier assumption: the "+2 ECM / −1 own fire" guess is gone.

## 3. Implemented
- B2/B3 sequence of play, energy allocation, life support, secret plans (B3.5), fractional accounting.
- C1-C4 movement, impulse chart, turn modes, sideslip; C5.1, C5.13, C5.2; C6 HET and breakdown; C7.1 and S2.2 disengagement by acceleration with the edge rule (SG1.41); C8; C9 and C10 as start-screen options; C11 partly (see 4).
- D2 arcs, D3 shields (minimum/full, reinforcement, repair D9.2), D4 damage allocation, D6.1-D6.6 sensors, scanners, EW, UIM, DERFACS, active fire control, D9.2 damage control, G9 crew (damage tracking), G13 cloak (most).
- E1-E5: phasers (P1, P2, P3, capacitors), disruptors with overloads, photons (arming, holding, overloads, proximity), ADD racks; F1-F3, FD1-FD4 (Type I, II, basic drone), FP1-FP4 (plasma R, S, G, F); H4-H7 power systems and reserve power; S2.2, S4.1.
- Ships: Federation CA, CC, DN, DD, CL (old, R2.5); Klingon C9, C8, D7, D6, F5, E4; Romulan K5R, WE, KR; Kzinti FF, CL, CS, BC, CC; Gorn CA, CL, DD, DDF; Tholian PC and PC+ (no web).

## 4. Partly implemented
- C11 nimble: EM cost, HETs, small-target ECM, and the turn-mode exemption are done; asteroid, quick-reverse and movement-order benefits are not (those rules are not in the game).
- C10 EM: guiding seeking weapons while erratic is not blocked; the one-hex-per-impulse equivalence of impulse power (C10.112) and EM with reserve power after deceleration (C8.43) are not modelled.
- C9 flywheel: braking/reversing, docking and uncontrolled ships do not occur in this game.
- G13 cloak: done in v119 apart from the small items in section 6i.
- D9 damage control: repairs from damage control points work; supplies, carryover, critical-hit repair and D9.7 are simplified.
- E5 anti-drones: ADD-6 and ADD-12 only. FD drones: Type I, II and basic drone only.
- Refits: Fed, Klingon, Romulan and Gorn done; Kzinti Y175 type-B/C racks not done.
- D5 explosions (v80): explosion strengths from the SSD EX boxes; see section 6. Not done: D5.3 beyond same-hex stacking, D5.51 crew restriction (needs friendly ships).
- S2 scoring: Standard Victory Conditions only (no Commander's Options, so no step B points); a ship dead in space ends the game and is scored as crippled, not destroyed.

## 5. Captain's Basic Set pass (v55)
Method: the 2012 Captain's Basic Rulebook was indexed (about 2,500 rule numbers), cross-references the Basic Set does not define (D22, C12, D17, D19 and others) were looked up in the Master Rulebook, and each rule that matters to a solo Duel (SG1.0) was compared with the game.

Added in v55:
- S2.20-S2.24 victory points: BPV difference to the smaller ship (lost if it leaves by the end of turn 2), 10/25/50/100% of the enemy BPV, only the greatest per ship, rounded half up; S2.3 levels of victory. Shown in the end-of-battle debrief, the log and Match history.
- S2.41 crippled status, all five conditions (A warp 10% or less, B half the interior boxes, C any excess damage, D all control spaces, E all weapons). It also now drives C6.522 (no first-HET bonus for a crippled ship).
- E2.251 low-power phaser fire: a Ph-1 or Ph-2 can fire as a Ph-3 for half a point of capacitor energy (range 15 or less), a tick box in the fire table.

Not applicable to the Duel (nothing to implement):
- C1.7 ramming (the rule says there is none); C1.6 stacking (no effect with two units).
- C7.2 separation (needs more than 50 hexes; the map is 42 by 30), C7.3 sublight evasion (no ship without warp in the roster), C7.4 automatic disengagement (monster scenarios only). SG1.41 edge disengagement is the Duel rule and is implemented.
- D1.5 friendly fire, C3.7 base rotation, E6 monsters, P terrain, M mines, J shuttles, G6 Klingon mutiny, S5/S6 local conditions and monsters, SG2-SG9 and SH scenarios.
- S3 balancing and Commander's Options (S3.2): the game has no point-buying; S4 weapons status is fixed at WS-II as SG1.2 says.

Left out on purpose (no effect in a Duel, or optional):
- D3.5 dropping shields mid-turn (only useful for transporters or saving power for the next turn), E2.32/H6.4 25-turn capacitor limit, E2.33 energizing at WS-0 (the Duel starts at WS-II).
- D6.7 low-power fire control (half a point; limits fire to range 15 and two weapons; meant for freighters).

Still open from the Basic Set (real rules, not yet built):
- D22 is optional in the book (the Basic Set only mentions it, C2.3/H1.0) and is now a start-screen option (v56), in simplified form: after damage the game compares power still needed with power left (spent power counts as coming from the destroyed boxes), then uses batteries, cancels unused HETs, EM and tactical maneuvers, and slows the ship by up to half (a disengaging ship only drops to its new maximum). Not modelled: emergency life support, phaser capacitor allocation, shield cuts, other warp uses.
- C3.5 reversing direction, C3.6 quick reverse, C5.4/C5.5 combined tactical maneuvers, C12 mid-turn speed changes.
- D9.5/D9.6 damage-control limits and carryover (D7, D8, G5, G7, G8 have since been built: see section 6).
- H2.5 emergency warp, H3.5 emergency impulse.
- E5.4/E5.7 ADD alternative ammunition and reloading, F2.4/F2.5 non-impact and mutual impact seeking weapons, FD2.5 dogfight drones, FD3 racks B-H, FD5 control methods, FP5-FP8 enveloping plasma, pseudo-plasma, shotgun and bolts.
- The AI does not use low-power phaser fire.

- Movement and EW: C3.5 reversing, C3.6 quick reverse, C12 mid-turn speed changes (not C12.24, C12.37), C5.4/C5.5 combined tactical maneuvers (not during stops), C10 erratic maneuvering, C11 nimble (now also the Orion CR).
- Weapons: E1.6 narrow salvoes (Advanced), FP2 proximity fuses, D3.6 leaky shields, FD3 type-C racks and two launches a turn, Type-VI dogfight drones, FP5 enveloping plasma, FP6 pseudo-plasma, FP8 plasma bolts.
- G7 tractor beams: drones and ships (auction, negative tractor, holder speed, held ship does not move or turn). Not done: G7.36 both moving, held-ship turning, G7.6 range, G7.7 rotation.
- G5 probes (launcher, two turns of warp energy, crippled-only unless agreed), G8 transporters and D7 boarding parties (casualty table, security stations, control-room capture; the AI never boards). Not done: D7.8 hit-and-run raids, capture handling beyond an instant victory.
- D8 critical hits (optional): fire control, battery, maneuver, warp controls, and now transporter (no beaming) and tractor (no beams; links and held drones released) results. Lab and shuttle results still have no effect because labs and shuttles are not used.
- G15 Orion Raider Cruiser (v77): SSD data, stealth ECM +2, lost while a warp engine is doubled (G15.8, G15.82); doubling of each warp engine with the box lost at the end of the turn (G15.2, G15.202, G15.212) and the 30-point limit (G15.26); fixed option mounts (three disruptor-22s). Not done: impulse doubling, Plus/cloak/OAKDISC refits.

Targets are chosen from a button row, each computer ship plans, moves, launches and fires; you win when every computer ship is out; S2.2 scoring sums them. Start-screen option for a second computer ship. Not done: more than one human ship, friendly computer ships, bases, terrain.
- Scenarios: SG1 (the Duel), SG5 (Duel with a Pirate Raider, normal map, no floating map) and, since v83, SG4 Basic Piracy (see 6c).

## 6b. D5 explosions and self-destruction (v80)
- New Advanced-level switch (16th flag, optBoom): Basic keeps the old game.
- Explosion strength (D5.2) is the EX box on each SSD: Fed DD 12, CL 14, CA 18, CC 18, DN 24; Klingon E4 8, F5 10, D6 18, D7 19, C8 31, C9 29; Romulan K5R 9, WE 13, KR 17; Gorn GDD 10, GDF 10, GCL 14, GCA 19 (SSD "Battlecruiser" assumed to be the game's Gorn heavy cruiser); Kzinti KFF 9, KCL 14, KCS 19, KBC 20, KCC 21; Tholian PC 8, PC+ 8; Orion CR 22. The uploaded Master Ship Chart has no explosion column.
- D5.41: strength 9 or less hits the hex only, 10 or more also the six surrounding hexes; each ship takes the full strength on the facing toward the blast (facing rolled in the same hex). D5.31: ships destroyed in the same hex add their strength; other-hex victims explode separately.
- D5.1 human self-destruct (two-step confirm in Energy Allocation, done at the start of the turn). G15.1/G15.12: an Orion AI that would concede self-destructs next turn instead (strength 22).
- Not done: D5.51 crew restriction (one ship per side, so it never applies), cloak interaction (D5.42 says it is ignored, which is what the code does).

## 6c. Freighters and SG4 Basic Piracy (v82 to v83)
- Ships (v82): Small Freighter F-S (R1.5) and Large Freighter F-L (R1.6) from the SSD book pages: box counts, shields 5 and 8 per facing, cargo 25 and 50, Ph-3 and Ph-2/Ph-3, explosion strength 2 and 5, BPV 26 and 61, turn mode C and D, movement cost 1/3 and 1/2. Each gains at most 3 or 4 movement points a turn and cannot disengage by acceleration (SSD notes). New "Merchant" side in the setup screen; they can be flown or fought.
- SG4 (v83, Advanced and up or any level): you fly the Orion Raider; the computer fields 3 to 6 freighters in the SG4.2 hexes heading B at speed 6, an optional escort (smallest warship of the chosen race, SG4.47) and, from turn 6, a die roll each turn for a relief cruiser (SG4.46, placed in the corner farthest from the convoy because the map is smaller than 35 hexes). Freighters plod at speed 6 and run when the Orion is within 16 hexes; they never concede and stay on the map when dead in space so they can be boarded (existing G8/D7 boarding captures them). Victory: the Orion loses if it captures no freighter (SG4.5); the convoy score adds 10 per large and 5 per small freighter less one third of the escort BPV; captured ships count as 100% in S2.2.
- Not done: playing the convoy (needs more than one human ship), floating map and disengaging in any direction (SG4.41), enforcing convoy cohesion (SG4.45; only a warning is logged), WS-0 start for the convoy, SG4.6 variations other than the Q-ship (armed freighters and other merchants need Advanced Missions SSDs), Orion LR.
- Asteroids (v84, option): P3.11 layout with the random drift, P3.12 asteroid hexes, P3.2/P3.21/P3.221 collision damage by speed on shield #1 (#4 in reverse, nimble -1), P3.33 +1 natural ECM per asteroid hex on the line of fire. Not done: P3.25 clearing a path, seeking weapons taking asteroid damage (P3.24, P3.32), P3.4 large asteroids (landing, bases on them), EM speed shift (P3.222), crew modifiers, AI avoidance of asteroid hexes.
- Base Station BS (v86, SSD R1.3): immobile unit with 30 shield boxes per facing, six Ph-4 and three Ph-3, 22 reactor boxes (APR and AWR counted together), explosion strength 11, BPV 120; the Ph-4 and its 13-range table were added (E2.14, 2 power per shot, range 100). The computer base rotates twice a turn (C3.7). Box counts came from pixel counts of the SSD page; the layout of the SSD is synthetic. Left out: hangar and PF modules, repair boxes, scout sensor channels, weapon option mounts W1 to W4, Y170/Y175 refit boxes, cloak, hit-and-run, docking. A base can be chosen as the opponent in the Duel (Bases side).
- SG6 Pursuit into the Asteroids (v86): you fly the Navy cruiser (any ship but Orion, freighters and bases); the computer flies the CR from 0517 and the base station in 4017; the asteroid field is forced on; any edge may be used to disengage. Scored by SG6.5 points (higher wins, a tie goes to the pirate). Not done: WS-III start for the CR and cruiser (all start at WS-II), SG6.6 and SG6.7 variations, BATS, two CRs.

## 9. Code check (v81)
Method: static scans of the single script (functions never referenced, `sh[1]` single-foe assumptions, the 16 option flags across presets, `lvFlags`, `setLevel` and the options UI), a rule-citation resolver (every code passed to a log line must resolve through RULES or the page index), and a headless soak test of 78 games (every ship, three rules levels, some with a third ship), checking for script errors, NaN or negative shields, and explosion state.
- Soak: 78 games, 76 finished within the cap, the 2 others were still going at the time cap (different pairings each run, so they are slow games, not hangs); 0 script errors, 0 bad-state findings.
- Flags: all 16 are present in the three presets, `lvFlags`, `setLevel` and have exactly one options row each; 15 further independent options are not preset-driven (leaky shields, D22, D8, dogfight drones, type-C reloads, rings, and so on) and are unchanged.
- Citations: 190 distinct codes are cited by log lines. Six did not resolve to a rulebook page (D6.51, D6.55, D6.521, G7.421, E5.61, G15.12); added to the page index with their pages. All 190 now resolve.
- D5 rule texts corrected against the master: D5.2 is "Self-destruction force" (not "explosion strength"), the Orion no-surrender rule is G15.11 (G15.12 is the suicide bomb), D5.12/D5.42 added, G15.1 text no longer says self-destruction is unmodelled; page numbers added for the D5 family.
- Dead code left in place: `fedCL` (an unused 3D model function) and `isCripOld` (an unused old-crippled check). No effect on play; remove in a later cleanup.
- Known limitations in reports (not rules): with a second computer ship, the turn records, debrief and match history still describe only the first computer ship, and shot statistics lump all computer ships together as "foe".
- Known behaviour: a very long game (CA against CR, the raider running with doubled engines) can run for dozens of turns; v79 behaves identically. It is the existing chase stalemate and the AI was not changed.
- Self-destruct test paths: forced Orion self-destruct next turn (game ends in a win), human self-destruct with two enemies (game ends in a defeat), same-hex combined explosion with damage to a second ship.

## 6d. Batch A (v89)

- E2.251: wStatus now lets a Ph-1/2/4 fire as a Ph-3 with only 1/2 point in the capacitor. Computer captain uses it when the option is on (capacitor short, or loses at most a quarter of the damage). Note: a Ph-3 never out-damages a Ph-1/2 at any range.

## 6e. Batch B (v90 to v92)
- C12.24 non-plotted acceleration (v90, needs the C12 mid-turn option): at a fire decision the player can pay reserve warp power to speed up. Cost is double the hexes gained, never more than speeding up for the rest of the turn, never less than one hex (times movement cost). Takes effect the impulse after it is announced; can be for the rest of the turn or end at a chosen impulse (C12.244). Limits kept: four speed changes a turn, eight impulses apart (six for nimble ships), the C12.33 acceleration limit, 30 warp points. Not done: unplotted acceleration with reserve impulse power (C12.25), and it is only offered when the game stops for a fire decision (raise "Stop for fire decisions at range" to stop earlier).
- C12.37 reversing direction through a plotted stop (v91): a speed change to zero followed eight impulses later (six if nimble) by a change with a "reverse" box flips the direction with no braking cost.
- C5.52/C5.532 tactical maneuvers during a plotted stop (v91): allowed for a halt; the maneuver is earned on impulse 8, 16 or 24 only after four impulses at zero (a ship stopped all turn still earns from impulse 2). Not done: C5.51 (after emergency deceleration, paid with reserve power) and sublight maneuvers with speed changes (C5.531).
- G7.6 extended tractor range and G7.7 rotation (v92, new Advanced option "Extended tractor range and rotation"): beams reach three hexes; effective strength is power divided by range (G7.61-G7.63), also in the tractor auction. A link kept into a new turn lets the human holder move the held ship one hex before movement (G7.71, G7.73, G7.74); the facing does not change. Not done: the simultaneous written rotation when both ships want to rotate (the computer never tractors), G7.718 bases, drones and shuttles at range.
- G7.36 both linked ships moving (pseudo-speed, delayed movement, both-return collisions): NOT done. It needs the pseudo-speed method, owed movement points carried between impulses and the same-hex collision cases; the game keeps its simplification that the held ship does not move on its own.

## 6f. Batch C (v93 to v95)
- D7.8 hit-and-run raids (v93, Commander's boarding switch): the human spends a transporter and a boarding party on one SSD box of the target (sensor, scanner, bridge, engines, batteries, tractor, transporters, and each phaser, tube, disruptor or plasma launcher). Same shield rules as beaming aboard (own shield drops, enemy facing shield must be down). Normal-crew chart: 1 system destroyed and party returns, 2 both destroyed, 3 to 5 party destroyed, 6 party returns and system fine. One raid per party per turn, one transporter each, 8 impulses between raids on one box (D7.84). Not done: Commando/Outstanding/Poor crew columns (crew quality is not modelled), guards (D7.83), raids by the computer, capture of items or people.
- FP7 plasma shotgun (v94, new Advanced option): a plasma-G, S or R can be finished on its last arming turn as a shotgun for double the last turn's energy; it splits into 2, 3 or 5 type-F torpedoes, each at a separate target in the launch arc, spare ones removed; cannot be held and cannot also be enveloping. It only has an effect with two or more computer ships. Not done: FP7.23 (two launchers in one impulse), FP7.24 bolts from a shotgun load, FP7.45 sensor blinding, the computer using shotguns.
- Orion CR refits (v95): Plus refit (Y169, +8 BPV) adds the shaded shield boxes read from the SSD page (shields become 24/24/20/20/20/24); Cloak refit (Y155, +18 BPV) gives a cloaking device with cost 10/2 and the G15.32 cost rise when warp engines are doubled (x2 for both, x1.5 for one). OAKDISC (Y127) not built: it only helps drone or plasma racks in option mounts, which the game does not fit. The SSD drawing does not show the extra Plus shield boxes.

## 6g. Batch D (v96 to v98)
- S1.43 floating map (v96, new option): a unit about to leave the map makes every ship, drone, asteroid (and web) move up to ten hexes away from that edge, columns by even numbers so hex rows still line up; the battle goes on. Disengagement still uses the authorized edge. Not used in SG6 (fixed map).
- G10 Tholian web (v97, new option; needs the asteroid option for anchors). The Tholian PC (it has a web generator; the PC+ does not) allocates "web energy" in energy allocation and chooses "lay web when I leave an asteroid hex". Done: laying (6 energy per hex, zero strength, G10.211), anchoring in asteroid hexes at both ends (G10.111-G10.113, G10.1162, G10.1314), 30-hex limit (G10.232), no crossing/touching other web hexes (G10.22), reinforcement 4 per ship per impulse when in or beside the web (G10.2121), strength = energy / hexes capped at 35 with fractions kept (G10.3, G10.31), deterioration 1 per hex per turn and dissolving after seven turns at zero (G10.41, G10.43), non-Tholian ships and seeking weapons caught on entry and freed after spending strength movement points within 32 impulses (G10.51, G10.511, G10.52), impact breakdown and shield damage at speed and strength 12 or more (G10.59-G10.592), drone damage over speed 20 (G10.593), anything launched or standing in a web hex is caught (G10.55), turning while caught (G10.57), no disengaging while caught, direct fire blocked through web hexes and Tholian phasers reduced by distance to the farthest web hex (G10.61, G10.62), tractors and transporters blocked through web hexes (G10.71), map floating moves webs too. Not done: globular webs (need two ships), extending or reshaping a web (G10.118), corner and intermediate anchors, web casters, anchor buoys, spinners, pulling a ship out with a tractor (G10.56), self-destruction blast reduction (G10.72), forgoing web passage (G10.533), year-based strength bonuses (G10.32, no year in the game), a straightness test beyond "each new hex touches only the last one" (G10.115). The computer Tholian does not lay web (AI unchanged); it is caught by webs the human lays.
- G7.36 two moving ships linked by a tractor (v98, inside the tractor option): pseudo-speed per ship = warp energy / total movement cost of both ships, plus its impulse point unless it is the smaller ship (or the ships tie: impulse ignored); each ship moves both ships on its own impulse chart and turns by its Turn Mode at the pseudo-speed; opposite movements on one impulse cancel; two movements 120 degrees apart combine into one hex; otherwise the larger ship moves first and the other movement is owed to the next impulse (queue carried across impulses and turns, lost if the link ends). Equal ships toss a coin. Replaces the old simplification that the held ship never moved.
